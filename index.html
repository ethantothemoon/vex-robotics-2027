Team Biography

We are Team 61434T, better known as Rising Sunset Robotics, a up and coming VEX V5 robotics team based in Irving, Texas. We engineer, build, innovate, program, and compete with teams all across the globe in the VEX V5 ROBOTICS COMPETITION. Through this experience we learn and improve our hard skills like coding and building while simultaneously strengthen our soft skills like teamwork, creativity and public speaking. To be honest we are just a group of teenagers that want to make our dreams come true.
