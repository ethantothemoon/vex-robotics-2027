About our robotics team

We’re a student‑led robotics team that designs, builds, and programs a new competition robot every season. Along the way, we learn engineering, software development, project management, and how to work as a tight‑knit team under real deadlines.

Engineering & build

From CAD to manufacturing, students own the full mechanical design process—iterating quickly and learning from every prototype.

Our 2025 robot: “Astra”

Every season starts with a blank page and a new game challenge. “Astra” was designed to be fast, consistent, and easy for drivers to control under pressure.

Drive base

6‑wheel west‑coast drive with high‑traction wheels and a low center of gravity for stability and quick turns.

  • Geared for speed and pushing power
  • Closed‑loop velocity control

Scoring mechanism

Dual‑arm intake and elevator system designed for fast game‑piece collection and precise placement at multiple heights.

  • Sensor‑based alignment
  • Preset scoring positions

Software & vision

Camera‑based vision and autonomous routines help the robot line up, score, and navigate the field with minimal driver effort.

  • AprilTag / target tracking
  • Multi‑path autonomous modes

Season highlights

Our season is more than just competition days—it’s months of design, testing, and teamwork. Here’s what a typical year looks like.

Kickoff & strategy

January

We study the new game, brainstorm strategies, and define robot priorities before touching any tools.

Design & prototyping

January – February

Subteams prototype mechanisms, test ideas, and finalize the robot design in CAD.

Build & programming

February – March

We manufacture parts, assemble the robot, and develop autonomous and tele‑op code.

Competition & outreach

March – April

We compete at regional events, iterate between matches, and share our work with the community.
