Team Biography

We are Team 61434T, better known as Rising Sunset Robotics, a up and coming VEX V5 robotics team based in Irving, Texas. We engineer, build, innovate, program, and compete with teams all across the globe in the VEX V5 ROBOTICS COMPETITION. Through this experience we learn and improve our hard skills like coding and building while simultaneously strengthen our soft skills like teamwork, creativity and public speaking. To be honest we are just a group of teenagers that want to make our dreams come true.

Think &
Teamwork

Over the course of the IQ season, MIX AND MATCH, our team earned a few awards. The most notable of these are the Teamwork 2nd Place Award and the Think Award. The Teamwork Award is the best score in The Final Match between two alliances in a single game, sadly we were only 10 points away from this award, but we earned 2nd in teamwork to earn the Teamwork 2nd Place award. The other is the Think Award which I talked about shortly in the Meet The Team Page. Well this award is given to the team with the highest autonomus(only code, no driving) skills score as well as the best code in general, this means that how it's written the notebook, how we talked about in the interview, and how we utilize it in our code.

Journey

Last year, we started off competing at the local level and ended our journey at the regional level in Texas Region 5. At our early competitions, we gained experience and identified weaknesses in our robot's performance and consistency. We made improvements to our design, tested new ideas, and worked together to make our robot more reliable and efficient. We were just one place away from qualifying for the World Championship in St. Louis, showing how much we improved and grew as a team.
